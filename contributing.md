## how you can help develop the app
- **suggest features**: open a [New Issue](https://github.com/pyr0lysis/pyrokan/issues/new) to share feature requests or ui improvements.
- **report bugs**: if something is wrong such as performance or not working right let me know by giving a step-by-step guide on how to reproduce the issue
